- Derive the failure condition from the f_2=5 summand in SRC-0004 Lemma 10.6, including the sign in gamma^*O(-5).

- Model ordered distinct reduced divisors as a finite etale S_6-cover of the reduced-support open in the relative linear system; do not call that open locus closed.
- Identify components, dimensions, stabilizers, and maps to tetragonal moduli.
