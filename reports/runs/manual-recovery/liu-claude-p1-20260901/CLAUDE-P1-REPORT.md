## VERDICT

**CONFIRMED** — conditional on the manuscript printing the four repairs listed under REQUIRED CHANGES. Every load-bearing step of Liu's route to $\mathcal M_{5,8}$ is either correct as written or repairable by an explicit, standard argument that I verified independently. ## CLAIM CHECKS

### (a) $A^*(\mathcal M_{5,8})=R^*(\mathcal M_{5,8})$ — holds

Traced end to end, not taken from Cor. 3.9.

**Independent locus.** Liu p. 3 presents $\mathcal M_{5,n}\smallsetminus\mathcal M^3_{5,n}$ as canonically embedded curves in a $\mathbf P^4$-*fibration* (not a projectivized bundle) — correct, since the Brauer obstruction is real. Prop. 2.1 (p. 4) is correct: $\deg(\mathcal O_C(-2)(\Gamma)\otimes\omega_C)=n-8$, so $n\le7$ is automatic; $n=8$ degenerates iff $\omega_C\cong\mathcal O(\sum p_i)$; the $n=9$ base-point argument is valid ($h^0(\omega(p))=h^0(\omega)=5$ by Riemann–Roch, so $p$ is a base point of $\omega(p)$, and base points of $\mathcal O(\sum_1^9p_i)$ lie in $\{p_i\}$). Max Noether applies since a complete intersection of three quadrics in $\mathbf P^4$ is exactly a non-trigonal, non-hyperelliptic canonical genus-5 curve ($\omega=\mathcal O_C(2\!+\!2\!+\!2\!-\!5)=\mathcal O_C(1)$, $\deg 8$).

Prop. 2.5 (pp. 5–7) is the one place Liu argues an open immersion carefully, including the smoothness locus via the $3\times3$ Jacobian minors (6). Dimension check: $\dim B\mathrm{SL}_5=-24$, $+4n$ for $(\mathbf P\mathcal V)^n$, $+3(12-n)$ for $G(3,\mathcal E)$ with $\operatorname{rk}\mathcal E=15-n$, total $12+n=\dim\mathcal M_{5,n}$. ✓ Lemma 2.7 (p. 7): $f_*\omega_f=\det(\mathcal S^\vee)\otimes\mathcal V^\vee$ with $c_1(\mathcal V)=0$ gives $\lambda_1=5c_1(\det\mathcal S^\vee)$ and $c_{2..5}(\mathcal V)$ polynomial in $\lambda$; $c_1(\eta_i^*\mathcal O(1))=\psi_i-\lambda_1/5$. ✓ Lemma 2.8 (p. 7) is correct although the GRR display (7) is mislabelled: the second term must be $R^1f_*\omega_f^{\otimes2}$, which vanishes by relative duality from $f_*\omega_f^\vee=0$ — Liu's sentence compresses exactly this. Cor. 2.9 ✓ for $n\le12$.

**Exceptional locus.** Prop. 3.1 (pp. 8–9): $\mathcal M_\omega$ is the rank-$\le4$ locus of $f_*\omega_f\to\bigoplus_1^8\sigma_i^*\omega_f$ ($e=5$, $f=8$, $k=4$), expected codimension $(e-k)(f-k)=4$, and the actual codimension is 4 because every fibre of $\mathcal M_\omega\to\mathcal M_5\smallsetminus\mathcal M^3_5$ is an open subset of $|\omega_C|=\mathbf P^4$ (with orderings): $\dim=12+4=16$ inside a 20-dimensional ambient. Porteous therefore applies, and the class is tautological. Rational coefficients absorb any determinantal multiplicity.

Lemma 3.2 (pp. 9–10) is correct and is the crux of the section. The residual exact sequence (11) gives $h^0(I_\Gamma(2))=3+\delta$ whenever $\Sigma$ is in linear general position, so the criterion is an "iff"; the case analysis (max plane containing $7,6,5,4,3$ points) is exhaustive, the $H\to H'$ swap in Case 4 is legitimate, and each of the three failure configurations forces $Q_1\cap Q_2\cap Q_3$ to contain a line, a conic, or a plane (the conic case needs Bézout plus the exclusion of 4 collinear points — Liu's parenthetical "but no 4 of them collinear" covers it). Hence $p_1,\dots,p_7$ always impose independent conditions and (12) is surjective.

Prop. 3.3/Cor. 3.4 (pp. 11–12): dimension check again closes exactly, $\dim BG=-20$, $+21$ for $(\mathbf PF)^7$, $+15$ for $G(3,\mathcal E)$ with $\operatorname{rk}\mathcal E=15-7=8$, total $16=\dim\mathcal M_\omega$. ✓ Prop. 3.5 is correct ($N^\vee_{D_i}=\sigma_i^*\omega_f$ gives $c_1(L)=2\psi_i$, hence all $\psi_i$ agree **on $\mathcal M_\omega$**). Cor. 3.6, Lemmas 3.7, 3.8: all generators of Cor. 3.4 are polynomials in $\psi_i,\lambda_j$, i.e. restrictions of tautological classes from the ambient; with $[\mathcal M_\omega]$ tautological, push–pull makes every class supported on $\mathcal M_\omega$ tautological. $\mathcal M_\omega$ is smooth (open in a Grassmann bundle), so $i^*$ is a ring map and ring generators suffice.

**Gluing.** $\mathcal M_{5,8}\supset\mathcal M^3_{5,8}$ with open complement $=U_8\sqcup\mathcal M_\omega$; Canning–Larson Lemma 9.9 (p. 33) needs $g\ge4$, $n\le g+7=12$, so it applies at $n=8$ and supplies both tautologicality of classes supported on $\mathcal M^3_{5,8}$ and its CKgP. Two excision sequences finish. **This step is not cited in Liu's Cor. 3.9** (only in his "Idea of the proof", p. 2); the manuscript supplies it correctly at line 420.

### (b) CKgP for $\mathcal M_{5,8}$ — holds

$B\mathrm{SL}_5$ (CL Lemma 3.8(2), p. 10) $\to$ iterated projective bundles (3.7) $\to$ open (3.3) $\to$ Grassmann bundle (3.7) $\to$ open (3.3) gives CKgP for $\mathcal M'_{5,8}$; the $\mu_5$-gerbe descent to $U_8$ needs **CL Lemma 3.6** (p. 10), which Liu does not cite. $BG\;(G=\mathbf G_a^4\rtimes\mathrm{GL}_4)$ needs **CL Lemma 3.5** (p. 9), which Liu *does* cite. $\mathcal M^3_{5,8}$ by CL Lemma 9.9. CL Lemma 3.4 (stratification) glues. Note the internal evidence that Liu knew what 3.5 was for: Prop. 4.3 (p. 15), where the stabilizer of $(H,p_9)$ is a Levi with no unipotent radical, drops 3.5 from the citation list and keeps the rest.

### (c) $W_{24}H^{24}(\mathcal M_{5,8};\mathbf Q)=0$ — holds

CLP Lemma 4.3 (p. 12) requires only "open substack of a smooth proper DM stack over $\mathbf C$" + CKgP; $\mathcal M_{5,8}\subset\overline{\mathcal M}_{5,8}$ qualifies. It yields $W_{24}H^{24}=\mathrm{cl}(A^{12}(\mathcal M_{5,8}))$; with (a), $=\mathrm{RH}^{24}$. Ionel Theorem 0.1 (p. 1): for $g\ge2$, $n>0$, any product of descendant/tautological classes of degree $\ge g$ vanishes in $H^*(\mathcal M_{g,n};\mathbf Q)$. Here $c=12\ge g=5$, with slack of 7. $R^*(\mathcal M_{g,n})$ is generated by $\psi,\kappa$ (the $\lambda$'s are $\kappa$-polynomials on the open stratum, as in Liu's Def. 1.1), so Ionel kills all of $R^{12}$. **Both halves of Liu are genuinely needed**: Lemma 4.3 alone gives only $\mathrm{cl}(A^{12})$, and Ionel only kills the tautological part.

### (d) Borel–Moore form in degree 16 — holds

$\dim_{\mathbf C}\mathcal M_{5,8}=3\cdot5-3+8=20$; $2d-24=16$. With the manuscript's convention (line 199) $H^{\mathrm{BM}}_i(X)\cong H^{2d-i}(X)(d)$, weights shift by $-2d=-40$, so weight 24 in $H^{24}$ ↔ weight $-16$ in $H^{\mathrm{BM}}_{16}$, and $-16$ is indeed the lowest weight occurring in $H^{\mathrm{BM}}_{16}$ of a smooth 20-fold. Hence $W_{-16}H^{\mathrm{BM}}_{16}\cong (W_{24}H^{24})(20)=0$. Dimension, degree and twist in the manuscript (lines 493–497) are all correct.

### Characteristic

Liu: algebraically closed, $\operatorname{char}\notin\{2,3,5\}$ (p. 3). Canning–Larson: "characteristic 0 or greater than 5". CLP Lemma 4.3: over $\mathbf C$. $\mathbf C$ satisfies all three, so the restriction is strictly weaker than the use — no gap, but the manuscript never says so.

## STRONGEST ATTACK

The most damaging line of attack is **Liu's Prop. 3.3 (p. 11)**, because it is the only load-bearing open-immersion claim in the paper that is asserted without proof ("Furthermore, $\mathcal M_\omega$ is isomorphic to an open locus $W$ in $G(3,\mathcal E)$"), and *both* conclusions depend on it: generation of $A^*(\mathcal M_\omega)$ needs excision surjectivity, and the CKgP needs $\mathcal M_\omega$ to be open in a Grassmann bundle. Unlike Prop. 2.5, no inverse map and no open conditions are constructed.

**It survives scrutiny.** The inverse exists: a net in $\mathcal E$ over a point of $V'$ cutting out a smooth complete intersection $C$ recovers $H$ from the $G$-structure, and $C\cap H=(Q_1\cap Q_2\cap Q_3)|_H$ is the length-8 scheme of Lemma 3.2, whose residual point is $p_8$; then $\mathcal O(\sum_1^8 p_i)=\mathcal O_C(1)=\omega_C$, so the object lies in $\mathcal M_\omega$. The needed loci (smooth complete intersection; $C\cap H$ reduced; $p_8\notin\{p_1,\dots,p_7\}$) are all open. The $G$-torsor is canonical: (10) trivializes $F'/F$, and $G\to\mathrm{Stab}_{\mathrm{PGL}_5}(H)$ is an isomorphism, so automorphism groups match and the monomorphism is an open immersion between smooth 16-dimensional stacks. Two independent dimension counts ($-20+21+15=16$ and $12+4=16$) close exactly, which is strong corroboration. Secondary attacks — that the diagonal in $(\mathbf PF)^7$ is not excluded (it is: coincident points make (12) non-surjective, so $V'$ misses it), and that $H$ might not be unique (it is: $h^0(\omega(-\Sigma))=1$) — also fail.

## Additional defects found (not in the local audit's list)

1. **Lemma 3.8 (p. 13) has a sign error.** With $\mathbf PK:=\operatorname{Proj}\operatorname{Sym}^\bullet K^\vee$, twisting $K$ by $L$ twists $\mathcal O(1)$ by $L^\vee$. Since $G^\vee=L\otimes G'^\vee$, the correct identity is $\mathcal O_{\mathbf PG^\vee}(1)=g^*\mathcal O_{\mathbf PG'^\vee}(1)\otimes a^*L^\vee$, giving $b_\omega^*\eta_i^*\mathcal O_{\mathbf PF}(1)=\sigma_i^*\omega_f\otimes L^\vee$ and $c_1=\psi_i-2\psi_i=-\psi_i$, not $3\psi_i$. Liu's own §2 analogue (Prop. 2.5 Claim, p. 5, and Lemma 2.7: $c_1=\psi_i+c_1(L_1)$) uses the correct convention; §3 flips it because the normalized bundle there is $f_*\omega_f\otimes L^\vee$, not $f_*\omega_f\otimes L$. **Harmless**: $-\psi_i$ is tautological.
2. **Prop. 3.1 (p. 9), Porteous indices are transposed.** In the convention of Eisenbud–Harris Thm. 12.4 ($\Delta^a_b=\det(c_{a+j-i})_{b\times b}$, class $=\Delta^{f-k}_{e-k}$), the correct symbol is $\Delta^4_1$, i.e. the degree-4 part — which is exactly the second displayed expression Liu actually uses. The written $\Delta^1_4$ denotes $s_{(1,1,1,1)}$, a different class. **Harmless**: both are tautological, and the used expression is the right one.

2. **Record that Liu's Cor. 3.9 does not itself cite the gonality input.** Line 420 already supplies CL Lemma 9.9; add half a sentence that this is the hyperelliptic+trigonal step ($n=8\le g+7=12$) which Liu states only in his introduction (p. 2), together with CL Thm. 6.1 for $n\le 2g+6=16$.
3. **Flag Prop. 3.3 as the one load-bearing step Liu asserts rather than proves,** and give the one-line repair: the inverse map sends a net cutting out a smooth complete intersection $C$ to $(C,p_1,\dots,p_7,p_8)$ with $p_8$ the residual point of $C\cap H$, so that $\mathcal O(\textstyle\sum_1^8p_i)=\mathcal O_C(1)=\omega_C$; openness is the smooth-complete-intersection, reducedness and distinctness conditions, exactly as in Liu's Prop. 2.5. 4. **Add the characteristic comparison** (one sentence): Liu works over an algebraically closed field of characteristic $\notin\{2,3,5\}$, Canning–Larson over characteristic $0$ or $>5$, and CLP Lemma 4.3 over $\mathbf C$; the use here is over $\mathbf C$, so the hypotheses are strictly satisfied and the $\mu_5$-gerbe descent is unobstructed.
